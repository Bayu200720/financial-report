Balance sheet

  
Cumulative position up to the end of the selected month, with increases and decreases netted. Total capital includes retained earnings (cumulative net profit) and equals the closing equity below. Assets, liabilities and capital do not affect profit and loss or cash flow. Your carousel card balances are included as assets: each opening amount is paired with Opening balance equity, and transactions that pick a card move both the card and the balance sheet.
